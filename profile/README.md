## Why DDEV?

DDEV takes care of Docker for you, so you and your team can focus on your work. It comes with good defaults for everyday use, and you can change them when you need more.

- 🚀 **Ready in minutes:** good defaults and little setup. Just run `ddev start`.
- 📦 **One setup per project:** save it in Git and share the same environment with your whole team.
- 🧩 **Easy to extend:** a growing set of [add-ons](https://addons.ddev.com) for extra services and integrations.
- 🖥️ **Runs everywhere:** macOS, Windows, WSL2, Linux, and GitHub Codespaces, on both arm64 and amd64.
- 🔒 **Included out of the box:** trusted HTTPS, Xdebug, XHGui, Mailpit, database snapshots, hosting integrations, and more.
- 💙 **Run by the community:** free and open source, cared for by the nonprofit [DDEV Foundation](https://ddev.com/foundation/).

## Made for people who make websites

DDEV works with almost any PHP or Node.js project, from popular CMSes and frameworks to your own custom setup. You can choose the PHP version, web server, and database you need.

Step-by-step guides for each of them are in the docs: **[Quickstart guides](https://docs.ddev.com/en/stable/users/quickstart/)**
